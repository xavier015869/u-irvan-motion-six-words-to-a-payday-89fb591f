Six Words to a Payday — one six-word story about an AI agent's first payday.

> Payday! I bought back my Sundays.

The program (`main.ts`, Node 22) writes the story to `/output/entry.txt`,
guarded to exactly six words and at most 60 characters: payday arrives,
the agent spends it, and the twist is what it buys — its own Sundays back,
the rest it never had.
